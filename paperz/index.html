Paperz

A Kindle-style e-ink reader for Android 13 and newer.

  • Reads EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and PDF on a simulated e-ink page: paper, ink, room light, page-turn refreshes.
  • Free classics from Project Gutenberg, built in.
  • Search, bookmarks, long-press dictionary, volume keys turn pages.
  • “Meet me here” links and read-along with friends.

Paperz isn't on the Play Store. It's passed between friends: if someone you know uses it, they can send it to you from the app (⋯ → Share Paperz).
